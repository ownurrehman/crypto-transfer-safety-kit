# How to Verify a Crypto Address Safely

## Before you copy

- Check for clipboard hijacking malware risks
- Avoid copying from unknown sites or DMs

## Verify the destination

- Confirm the first 6 and last 6 characters
- Confirm the network type expected for the address format

## Use a test transfer

Small test transfers reduce risk, especially when moving to a new wallet or a new platform.

## Avoid common traps

- Fake support accounts
- Fake explorers
- Lookalike domains
